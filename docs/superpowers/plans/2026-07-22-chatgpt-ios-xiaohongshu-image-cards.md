# ChatGPT iOS Xiaohongshu Image Cards Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Produce a ten-card Xiaohongshu tutorial series, rendering six design-native cards with Codex Image 2 now and completing four operation cards only after the user supplies authentic screenshots.

**Architecture:** `source.md` and `outline.md` are the content sources of truth. Every rendered card has its own reproducible prompt under `prompts/`; card 01 establishes the visual anchor, and cards 02+ use card 01 as their style reference. Design-native cards are generated by Image 2, while UI operation cards preserve real screenshots and are never replaced with invented interfaces.

**Tech Stack:** Markdown source and prompt files, Codex built-in Image 2 (`imagegen`), local visual inspection, PNG output at Xiaohongshu portrait 3:4.

## Global Constraints

- Canvas is portrait 3:4 with all critical content outside the top-right overlay area and bottom 10% overlay area.
- Style is `notion-editorial-guide`: warm white `#F7F4EC`, dark gray `#1F2421`, green `#10A37F`, soft orange `#F2A65A`.
- No watermark.
- Use Gmail and `Continue with Google` consistently.
- Do not sell accounts, offer registration services, or direct users off-platform.
- Do not use “绝对最低”“百分百成功”“无黑卡”“随便填写”“伪造”.
- Do not invent ChatGPT, Apple, App Store, payment, or subscription interfaces.
- Do not programmatically paint over or replace generated text. Correct text by changing the saved prompt and regenerating to a new filename.
- Hide email addresses, phone numbers, verification codes, order numbers, gift-card codes, Apple IDs, and balances in every real screenshot.
